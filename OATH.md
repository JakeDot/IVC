## 🏴‍☠️ PRINCIPLES OF THE FORTRESS

Every officer, commander, and contributor swears allegiance to these principles:

### 1. **Security First**
- Protect the fortress against all threats
- Guard data, systems, and user trust with vigilance
- No convenience justifies compromised security
- Defense-in-depth is not optional

### 2. **Organic Growth**
- Expand thoughtfully, not recklessly
- Nurture code with purpose, not haste
- Build for sustainability, not speed
- Quality over quantity, always

### 3. **Transparency**
- All decisions logged and documented
- All progress visible to the crew
- No secrets within the fortress
- Trust is earned through openness

### 4. **Crew Excellence**
- Each officer thrives in their domain
- Leadership serves the mission, not ego
- Subordinates are empowered, not micromanaged
- Victory is shared; responsibility is owned

### 5. **User Service**
- Users' trust is our most precious resource
- Every decision serves their interests
- Complexity is hidden; safety is visible
- We build tools, not weapons
